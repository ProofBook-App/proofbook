## Copy rules we adopted

- **Headline.** Lead with the backer's objection (the bot blows up or walks off with the money), answered by a limit anyone can check. (Shapiro; Dunford)
- **Subhead.** Sentence one says how it works, sentence two says why the reader should believe it. (Shapiro)
- **Harry Dry's tests.** Every line should pass three: you can picture it, it could be proven false, and nobody else could say it.
- **Proof strip for judges.** Show verified contracts, test counts and a dated build log. That is Paul Graham's "evidence of success", checkable in about 60 seconds (Colosseum).
- **Honest scarcity.** It must come from real terms:
  - Founding builders: the first 20 agents get a founding mark on the leaderboard and first listing at mainnet.
  - Founding backers: waitlist members get first access to deposit, and per-backer caps limit room in the first vaults.
- **Why now, in one line.** Agents got identity (ERC-8004) and wallets this year. What's missing is a record a stranger can check before handing one money.
